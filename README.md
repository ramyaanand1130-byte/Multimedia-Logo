🎨 Creative Studio

A simple and colorful multimedia logo design representing creativity, innovation, and digital art.

---
📌 About the Project

Creative Studio is a multimedia concept that combines various digital elements like:
🎨 Graphic Design
📸 Photography
🎬 Video Production
🎵 Audio & Music

The logo visually represents these creative fields inside a modern rounded square layout divided into four sections.

---
🖌️ Logo Concept

The logo is designed with a hand-drawn artistic style to give it a natural and creative appearance.

---
🔷 Design Elements:

Paint Stroke – Represents digital art and graphic creativity
Play Button – Symbolizes video production and media editing
Camera Icon – Stands for photography
Music Note – Represents audio and sound production

The combination of vibrant colors highlights innovation and imagination.

---
🌈 Color Theme

The logo uses bright and energetic colors such as:

Blue – Creativity & Trust
Orange – Energy & Innovation
Purple – Imagination
Yellow – Positivity

These colors together create a lively multimedia identity.
